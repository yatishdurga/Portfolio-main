About Me

The story behind the data

I'm a Data Scientist & AI Engineer with 4+ years of experience turning complex data into intelligent, production-ready systems. My journey started in software development, evolved through data engineering at GAVS Technology, and now spans the full spectrum from building ETL pipelines to architecting RAG-powered AI systems.
